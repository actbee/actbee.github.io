自己紹介

Hi! My name is Xuedan Zou (Daniel/Dan). I am currently a software engineer at Rakuten Group based in Setagaya-ku, Tokyo, Japan.
I did my graduate school at Dartmouth College with a focus on Digital Arts, Computer Science. Before, I finished my bachelor study both in Computer Science and Industrial Design at Hunan University. During my graduate study at Dartmouth College, I was advised by Prof. Lorie Loeb and Prof. Elizabeth L. Murnane. My thesis, chosen as one of the Arts Integration Initiative projects by Hopkins Center for the Arts at Dartmouth College, explores new relationships among artist, audience, and artwork in exhibition environments.
My artworks were exhibited in multiple places like New Asian Generation Design Exhibition(2020), IV23 & CGiV23 Online Exhibition (2023), Junction Arts&Media(2023), Dartmouth College(2023) and AVA Gallery and Art Center(2023).
In my spare time, I continue my art life by doing artworks and writing novels/articles. I am a member of studio アトリエ４１５ and advised by Koji Suganuma sei-sei. For my writings, you can find them on the blog page of this site or directly visit My Substack. Besides, I post photos on Instagram.

If you are interested in any of my work, feel free to contact me directly!
