自己紹介

Hi! My name is Xuedan Zou (Daniel/Dan). I am a software engineer at Rakuten Group and an independent creator, based in Setagaya-ku, Tokyo, Japan.

I did my graduate studies at Dartmouth College, with a focus on Digital Arts and Computer Science. Before that, I completed a bachelor's degree in both Computer Science and Industrial Design at Hunan University. At Dartmouth, I was advised by Prof. Lorie Loeb and Prof. Elizabeth L. Murnane. My thesis, selected as one of the Arts Integration Initiative projects by the Hopkins Center for the Arts at Dartmouth College, explores new relationships among artist, audience, and artwork in exhibition environments.

My artworks have been exhibited at the New Asian Generation Design Exhibition (2020), the IV23 & CGiV23 Online Exhibition (2023), Junction Arts & Media (2023), Dartmouth College (2023), and the AVA Gallery and Art Center (2023).

Outside of my daily work, I continue to make art and to write novels and essays. I am a member of Studio アトリエ415, where I am advised by Koji Suganuma sensei. My writing can be found on the blog page of this site or on my Substack, and I post photos on Instagram.

If you are interested in my work, feel free to get in touch.
